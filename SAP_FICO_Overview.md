# SAP FICO Overview

SAP FICO is a commonly used term for two SAP finance areas:

## Financial Accounting (FI)
FI supports recording and organising financial transactions and producing information for external reporting. Common areas include General Ledger, Accounts Payable, Accounts Receivable, Asset Accounting and bank-related accounting.

## Controlling (CO)
CO supports internal management accounting. It helps organisations understand where costs occur, compare actual costs with plans, and analyse cost centres, internal orders and profitability.

## Integration
Finance data can connect with business activities in purchasing (MM), sales (SD), production (PP), human resources and projects. The exact process depends on the SAP product version and system configuration.

## Benefits
- Centralised financial information
- Better visibility into financial and cost data
- More structured processes
- Support for reporting and management decisions
- Cost monitoring and audit support

## Challenges
- Implementation and training costs
- Configuration complexity
- Data migration and data quality
- User training and change management
- Integration, security and ongoing support
