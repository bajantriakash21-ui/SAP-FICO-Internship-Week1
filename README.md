# SAP FICO Internship – Week 1

## Project title
Understanding SAP FICO: Financial Accounting and Controlling

## Overview
This repository contains the Week 1 research work for an introductory SAP FICO internship task. It explains the purpose of SAP Financial Accounting (FI) and Controlling (CO), their common functions, integration with other SAP business areas, benefits, and implementation challenges.

**Project type:** Research and learning documentation  
**Practical implementation:** This repository does not claim to configure or implement a live SAP system.

## Problem statement
Many organisations manage large numbers of financial transactions, supplier payments, customer receipts, budgets, and cost details. Disconnected tools or highly manual processes can lead to duplicated data, errors, delays, and limited visibility into costs. An integrated finance system can support structured financial recording, reporting, and internal cost monitoring. SAP FICO provides FI and CO capabilities that can help address these needs when properly configured.

## Objectives
1. Understand the basic meaning and purpose of SAP FICO.
2. Study the main functions of Financial Accounting (FI).
3. Understand Controlling (CO) and internal cost management.
4. Identify integration with other SAP business modules.
5. Study benefits of an integrated finance system.
6. Identify common implementation and operational challenges.
7. Understand basic business examples of SAP FICO.

## Topics covered
- SAP and ERP introduction
- Financial Accounting (FI)
- Controlling (CO)
- General Ledger, Accounts Payable, Accounts Receivable and Asset Accounting
- Cost Centres, Internal Orders and Profitability Analysis
- Integration with Materials Management (MM), Sales and Distribution (SD), Production Planning (PP), HCM and Project System
- Basic supplier purchase-to-payment process
- Benefits, challenges and applications
