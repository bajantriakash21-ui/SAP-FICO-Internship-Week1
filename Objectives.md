# Objectives of the Study

1. To understand the basic meaning and purpose of SAP FICO.
2. To study the main functions of SAP Financial Accounting (FI).
3. To understand the role of SAP Controlling (CO) in internal cost management.
4. To identify how SAP FICO connects with other SAP business modules.
5. To study the major benefits of using an integrated finance system.
6. To identify common implementation and operational challenges.
7. To understand basic business examples where SAP FICO may be used.
