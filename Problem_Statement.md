# Problem Statement

Many organisations must manage financial transactions, accounting records, supplier payments, customer receipts, budgets, and cost details. When these activities are handled through disconnected tools or highly manual processes, data may be duplicated, errors may occur, reports may take longer to prepare, and managers may not receive timely cost information.

These issues can make financial control and decision-making difficult. Therefore, organisations need an integrated and structured system that supports accurate financial recording, reporting, and internal cost monitoring. SAP FICO provides Financial Accounting (FI) and Controlling (CO) capabilities that can help address these needs when it is properly configured and used.
